The Story

Obsessed with the game.
Ready to share it.

I've spent years playing competitive beach volleyball, studying the game obsessively, and building tools to understand what separates good players from great ones.

Now I'm channeling that obsession into coaching and technology. Whether you're looking to sharpen your competitive edge or just want your pickup games to actually count, I'm building the tools to make it happen.

I'm the creator of Beach Volley Vision — an AI platform that automates scoring, generates highlights, and feeds results directly into VolleyballLife and TruVolley. I'm also running a decentralized DC volleyball league where your pickup matches become officially rated.
