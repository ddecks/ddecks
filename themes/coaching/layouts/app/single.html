The Format

How the league works.

Register — Sign up through VolleyballLife (DDVB)
Play — Schedule your own matches with other registered players. Minimum 6 per month.
Record (ideal) — Set up a phone and let BVV process the match. Not required but gives you video verification + highlights.
Report — Submit your score. If you recorded, BVV auto-extracts it.
Get Rated — TruVolley updates your rating overnight based on results and opponent strength.
Playoffs — Top-rated players at end of month play a KOB. Winner takes bragging rights (and pot if opted in).
