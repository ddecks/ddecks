DC Volleyball League

Pickup with purpose.
Your own shadow pro tour.

A decentralized rating system for the DC/VA beach volleyball scene. No fixed schedule. No single tournament day. Just play your matches throughout the month, report scores, and let ELO do the rest.

📈 ELO Rating System

TrueVolley-style ratings based on who you play and how you perform. Strength of schedule matters — beat strong teams, climb faster.

  • Play any sanctioned opponent
  • Minimum 6 matches per month
  • Ratings update after each match
  • Monthly standings & history

🗓️ Your Schedule

No coordinator needed. You and your opponents arrange your own matches — any court, any day, any time. Fully decentralized.

  • Play on your own schedule
  • Any court in DC/VA
  • Minimize commute time
  • Weekday or weekend

🏆 Monthly Playoffs

End-of-month playoff for top-rated players. King of the Beach format. Bragging rights (and maybe a pot if people want it).

  • Top players qualify by rating
  • KOB or bracket format
  • Optional buy-in / refund model
  • Points toward national ranking
